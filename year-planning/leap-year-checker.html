Leap Year Checker

Check whether a year has 365 or 366 days using the Gregorian calendar rules.

How it works

A Gregorian leap year is divisible by 4, except century years must also be divisible by 400.

Results are calculated in your browser and update when you change an input. For date-based results, your device’s local calendar date is used unless a different date is selected.
