Date Difference Calculator

Find the exact calendar difference between two dates, including total days and approximate weeks.

The total-day count uses elapsed calendar days; the start date is not counted as a full elapsed day.

How it works

The calculator compares the two calendar dates and reports the elapsed number of days, plus a week-based breakdown. The order of the dates does not change the absolute difference.

Results are calculated in your browser and update when you change an input. For date-based results, your device’s local calendar date is used unless a different date is selected.
