Time Until a Clock Time

Calculate how long remains until a selected time today, or tomorrow if that time has already passed.

If the selected time has already passed today, the tool counts to that time tomorrow.

How it works

The selected clock time is compared with the current local time. When the time has passed for today, the next occurrence is tomorrow.

Results are calculated in your browser and update when you change an input. For date-based results, your device’s local calendar date is used unless a different date is selected.
