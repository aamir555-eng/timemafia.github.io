Time Zone Converter

Convert a date and time between IANA time zones using your browser’s built-in time-zone database.

Daylight-saving rules are applied by your browser. This tool converts a local wall-clock time; ambiguous or nonexistent times around clock changes may require checking the source zone’s rules.

How it works

The converter uses the browser’s internationalization time-zone data to display the chosen moment in another zone. Time-zone offsets can vary by date because of daylight-saving rules.

Results are calculated in your browser and update when you change an input. For date-based results, your device’s local calendar date is used unless a different date is selected.
