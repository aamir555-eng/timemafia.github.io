Days Until a Date

Calculate how many days remain until a date, or how long ago a past date occurred.

Past dates are shown as time elapsed, rather than negative countdowns.

How it works

The target date is compared with today’s local date. A future target shows days remaining; a date in the past shows how many days have elapsed.

Results are calculated in your browser and update when you change an input. For date-based results, your device’s local calendar date is used unless a different date is selected.
